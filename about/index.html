The work

What One Way cleans

The company does four kinds of work:

  • Window cleaning for houses, inside and out or exterior only. Frames, sills and screens are included.
  • Hard water removal for the white mineral spots that sprinklers and weather leave on glass. The job starts with a test spot, and etching can be improved, not always fully restored. That gets said before the work, not after.
  • Pressure washing for the hard surfaces around a home: driveways, walkways, patios, steps and entryways.
  • Gutter cleaning, cleared by hand, with a downspout flow check and the debris taken care of.

Most homes book the work in Everett, Snohomish, Seattle or Bellevue, and the company works everywhere in between those cities too. One visit can cover several jobs at once. The pricing and cost guide explains what each service usually costs around here.

Hiring anyone

What to look for when hiring a window cleaner

You do not have to hire One Way for this part to be useful. A few plain questions separate a careful cleaner from a careless one:

  • What exactly is included? Glass only, or frames, sills and screens too? Get it in plain words before the work.
  • Is the price set before work starts? A real price, agreed up front, beats a low number that grows at the door.
  • Who actually shows up? Knowing whether you are dealing with the owner, a crew or a subcontractor tells you who answers for the work.
  • What happens if something is wrong? Ask before the job, while it is still a friendly question.

One Way's answers: frames, sills and screens are included in a standard window clean; the price is set before any work starts; and when you call, you talk to the owner, who is the person responsible for the work.
